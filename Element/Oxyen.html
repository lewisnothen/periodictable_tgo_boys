Oxygen

Oxygen

Oxygen (O) is a non-metal chemical element with an atomic number of 8 and an atomic mass of about 16.00 u. It belongs to Group 16 and Period 2 of the periodic table. At room temperature, oxygen is a colourless, odourless gas that makes up about 21% of Earth's atmosphere. It is essential for respiration and supports combustion.

Physical Properties

Data
  • State: Gas at room temperature
  • Colour: Colourless
  • Density: About 1.43 g/L
  • Melting Point: About −219°C
  • Boiling Point: About −183°C
🧪

Chemical Properties

See the color, test the mass, solid, liquid, or a gas!

Data
  • Highly reactive compared with nitrogen.
  • Reacts with many elements to form oxides.
  • Supports combustion, although it is not itself a fuel.
  • Combines with hydrogen to form water (H₂O).
  • Can form ozone (O₃) under certain conditions.

Discovery

Carl Wilhelm Scheele , Joseph Priestley & Antoine Lavoisier

Oxygen was independently discovered by Carl Wilhelm Scheele around 1772 and Joseph Priestley in 1774. Antoine Lavoisier later recognized oxygen as an element and helped establish its role in combustion and respiration.


Usages

Medical oxygen

🏥 Helps patients who need additional oxygen.

Space exploration

🚀 Used as an oxidizer in rocket systems.

Diving

🤿 Used in specialized breathing systems.

Interesting Facts

Atmosphere

🌬️ Oxygen makes up about 21% of Earth's atmosphere.

Need Oxygen

🫁 Humans and many other organisms need oxygen for cellular respiration.

Photosynthesis

🌱 Plants release oxygen during photosynthesis.

Combustion

🔥 Oxygen supports burning, making fires burn more easily.

Liquid Oxygen

💧 Liquid oxygen has a pale blue colour.
