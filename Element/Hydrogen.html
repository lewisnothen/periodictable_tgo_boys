Hydrogen

Hydrogen

Hydrogen is the lightest and most abundant chemical element in the universe, and hydrogen is the first chemical element on the periodic table, with the symbol H and atomic number 1.

Properties

📖

Physical Properties

See the color, test the mass, solid, liquid, or a gas!

Data
  • State: Gas at room temperature
  • Colour: Colourless
  • Density: Very low; it is the lightest element
  • Melting point: About − 259°C
  • Boiling point: About − 253°C
🧪

Chemical Properties

See the color, test the mass, solid, liquid, or a gas!

Data
  • Flammability: Highly flammable and burns easily in air or oxygen
  • Combustion: Reacts with oxygen to form water (H₂O)
  • Reaction with metals: Reacts with some reactive metals to form metal hydrides
  • Combustion product: Produces water when completely burned in oxygen
  • Oxidation state: Usually +1; −1 in metal hydrides

Discovery

Henry Cavendish

British scientist Henry Cavendish discovered hydrogen in 1766 by pouring acids onto metals (zinc, iron, and tin). He collected the rising gas, discovered it was highly flammable and lighter than air, and later proved that burning it produced water.


Useages

fertilizer manufacturing

Chemical factories combine hydrogen gas with nitrogen to synthesize ammonia, which serves as the foundational ingredient for agricultural fertilizers worldwide.

Petroleum Refining

Oil refineries inject hydrogen into crude oil during processing to strip away harmful sulfur impurities, resulting in cleaner-burning gasoline and diesel fuels.

Aerospace Propulsion

Because of its immense energy output when burned with oxygen, liquid hydrogen is used as the primary rocket fuel to launch spacecraft into orbit.

Interesting Facts

Found in water

💧 Every water molecule (H₂O) contains two hydrogen atoms.

Very light gas

🎈 Hydrogen is much lighter than air, which is why it can make balloons rise.

First element

🧪 Hydrogen has atomic number 1, making it the first element on the periodic table.

Important for life

🫀 Hydrogen is found in many important substances in living organisms, including water and organic compounds.

Found in the human body

🧬 Hydrogen is present in water and molecules such as carbohydrates, fats, and proteins.
