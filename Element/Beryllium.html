Beryllium

Beryllium

Beryllium is a lightweight, strong, steel-gray metal with the chemical symbol Be and atomic number 4. It belongs to Group 2 of the periodic table and is classified as an alkaline earth metal. Beryllium has a high melting point and is valued for its light weight, stiffness, and ability to withstand high temperatures.

Properties

📖

Physical Properties

See the color, test the mass, solid, liquid, or a gas!

Data
  • Hard, lightweight, silvery-white metal.
  • Low density: 1.85 g/cm³.
  • High melting point: 1287°C.
  • Appearance: Shiny and metallic
  • Electrical Conductivity: Good conductor of electricity
🧪

Chemical Properties

See the color, test the mass, solid, liquid, or a gas!

Data
  • Forms mainly Be²⁺ ions.
  • Reacts less readily than other Group 2 metals.
  • Its oxide, BeO, is amphoteric.
  • Bonding: Beryllium commonly forms covalent compounds because its small size and high charge density strongly attract electrons.
  • Reaction with water: Does not normally react with water

Discovery

Louis Nicolas Vauquelin

Beryllium was discovered by Louis Nicolas Vauquelin, a French chemist, in 1798. He discovered it while studying the minerals beryl and emerald. At first, he named the new element “glucinium” because some of its compounds had a sweet taste.


Usages

X-ray equipment

🩻 Used for X-ray windows because X-rays can pass through beryllium easily.

Automotive Parts

🚗 Beryllium-copper alloys can be used in specialized parts that require strength and durability.

Electrical equipment

⚡️ Used in beryllium-copper alloys for electrical connectors, switches, and springs.

Interesting Facts

High Melting Point

🌡️ Beryllium melts at about 1,287°C, which is high for such a lightweight metal.

Rare Element

Beryllium is relatively rare in Earth's crust and is usually obtained from minerals such as beryl.

Toxic Dust

⚠️ Beryllium and some of its compounds can be harmful if their dust or fumes are inhaled, so it must be handled carefully.

Useful in Nuclear Technology

⚛️ Beryllium can help reflect and interact with neutrons, making it useful in certain nuclear applications.

Good Thermal Properties

🧲 Beryllium conducts heat well, which makes it useful in some high-performance electronic and aerospace components.
