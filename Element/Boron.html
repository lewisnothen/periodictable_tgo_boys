Boron

Boron

Boron is a chemical element with the symbol B and atomic number 5. It is a metalloid, meaning it has properties of both metals and non-metals. Boron is a hard and brittle solid with a dark appearance. It is not usually found as a pure element in nature but is found in minerals such as borax. Boron is useful in making products such as heat-resistant glass, fibreglass, and detergents.

Properties

📖

Physical Properties

Data
  • State: Solid at room temperature
  • Colour: Dark brown or black
  • Density: About 2.34 g/cm³
  • Melting point: About 2077°C
  • Boiling point: About 4000°C
🧪

Chemical Properties

See the color, test the mass, solid, liquid, or a gas!

Data
  • Oxidation States: Mainly +3
  • Reaction with Acids: Generally resistant to many non-oxidizing acids.
  • Reactivity: Relatively unreactive at room temperature but reacts with several elements at high temperatures.
  • Bonding: Boron commonly forms strong covalent compounds rather than simple ionic compounds.
  • Combustion: When strongly heated, boron can burn with a bright green flame and produces boron oxide.

Discovery

Joseph Louis and Louis J Thénard and Humphry Davy

Boron was discovered in 1808 by Humphry Davy and, independently, Joseph Louis Gay-Lussac and Louis Jacques Thénard. Their work successfully produced a new element from boron compounds, establishing boron as a distinct chemical element.


Usages

Detergents

🧼 Boron compounds such as borax are used in some cleaning products.

Wood Preservation

🪵 Some boron compounds help protect wood from insects and fungi.

Fertilizers

🌱 Boron is an essential micronutrient that helps plants grow properly.

Interesting Facts

A Metalloid

🪨 Boron has properties of both metals and non-metals.

Green Flame

🟢 When heated strongly, some boron compounds can produce a bright green flame

Very Hard

💎 Crystalline boron is an extremely hard material.

Boron Makes Heat-Resistant Glass

Boron compounds are used in borosilicate glass, which can handle sudden temperature changes better than ordinary glass.

Boron Helps Make Powerful Magnets

🧲 Boron is used in neodymium-iron-boron (NdFeB) magnets, which are found in speakers, headphones, electric motors, and other devices.
