Nitrogen

Nitrogen

Nitrogen (N) is a non-metal chemical element with an atomic number of 7 and an atomic mass of about 14.01 u. It belongs to Group 15 and Period 2 of the periodic table. At room temperature, nitrogen is a colourless, odourless gas and exists mainly as N₂ molecules. It makes up about 78% of Earth’s atmosphere, making it the most abundant gas in the air.

  • Does not support combustion.
  • Has oxidation states from −3 to +5./li>

Discovery

Daniel Rutherford

Nitrogen was discovered by Scottish physician and chemist Daniel Rutherford in 1772. He showed that a component of air could not support combustion or respiration. It was later recognized as the element we now call nitrogen. The name nitrogen comes from Greek roots meaning “nitre-forming.”


Usages

Food packaging

🥫 Nitrogen gas can be used to help protect certain foods from oxidation.

Aircraft and vehicles

✈️ Nitrogen can be used in some specialized tire applications.

Fertilizers

🌾 Used to make ammonia and nitrogen fertilizers that help plants grow.

Interesting Facts

Atmosphere

🌬️ Nitrogen makes up about 78% of Earth's atmosphere.

Growth

🌱 Plants need nitrogen to grow, but most plants cannot directly use atmospheric N₂.

Food

🍗 Nitrogen is found in many foods because proteins contain nitrogen.

Essential

🧬 Nitrogen is essential for DNA, proteins, and other important molecules in living things.
