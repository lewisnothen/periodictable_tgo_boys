More About Periodic Table


What is Periodic Table?

You Know the Table. Discover What’s Behind It.

Explanation
  • The Periodic Table is a chart that organizes all the known chemical elements in a systematic way. It helps scientists and students understand the properties of elements and predict how they will react with other elements.
  • There are 118 known elements in the Periodic Table. They are arranged in order of their atomic number, which is the number of protons in the nucleus of an atom.


Type of Groups

Group 1: Alkali metals — Very reactive
Group 2: Alkaline earth metals
Group 17: Halogens — Reactive non-metals
Group 18: Noble gases — Mostly unreactive
